SCHOOL OF ARTS AND SCIENCES

Vision and Mission

VISION

In consonance with the Catholic identity of the University of the Assumption as an evangelized and evangelizingcommunity, the School of Arts and Sciences envisions itself to be a Christ-centered institution whose faculty, students and graduates manifest excellence in intellectual pursuit (Scientia): unwavering conscience for moral aspirations (Virtus): and unparalleled drive for social leadership and responsibility (Communitas).

MISSION

The School of Arts and Sciences commits itself into the integral development of Catholic leaders and seeks to produce world class graduates who are: BIASA (academically competent), MAGANACA (morally upright), and MAYAP (socially responsible).
