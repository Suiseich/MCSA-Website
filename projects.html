RADIO MARIAE

“It has been more than ten years of fostering and nurturing the spirit of AB Communication! On 𝗗𝗲𝗰𝗲𝗺𝗯𝗲𝗿 𝟵, 𝟮𝟬𝟭𝟬, Radio Mariae held its maiden broadcast, mobilizing UA students to take part in producing a weekly radio show. The official airing with regular programming began on 𝗝𝗮𝗻𝘂𝗮𝗿𝘆 𝟭𝟮, 𝟮𝟬𝟭𝟭, transforming it from a simple training ground for broadcasters to an informative and entertaining platform for students through segments like Heart Talk and Kumustahan.

With a strong commitment to promoting Kapampangan culture and university initiatives, Radio Mariae plays a crucial role in fostering a sense of community on campus. Our student trainees have stepped up, crafting scripts and producing shows that resonate with their peers. As we continue to evolve into the digital space with platforms like YouTube and Spotify, we invite everyone to tune in and celebrate the voices that shape our community! Here’s to many more years of learning, growth, and connection!”
